Privacy Policy

Car Tow Jam: Parking Puzzle · Last updated September 30, 2026

This policy explains what information is used when you play Car Tow Jam: Parking Puzzle (the "App"), published by Kinex Apps ("we", "us"). If you have questions, email muhammadharisgift1@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, contacts or location, and we do not run our own servers that collect data about you. The App stores your game progress, coins, shop items and settings only on your device. Deleting the App deletes this data.

We do not use analytics services in the App.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Free players see ads, which are provided by Google AdMob. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information from your device such as the advertising identifier (IDFA), IP address, device and app information, approximate location derived from the IP address, and how you interact with ads.

  • App Tracking Transparency: on iOS, the App asks for permission before the advertising identifier can be used for tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are still shown but are not personalised with that identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): where required by law, the App shows Google's consent message before any personalised ads are served, and you can choose your preferences.

Learn more in Google's policies: How Google uses information in ads and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions are purchased through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see or store your payment details. The App only receives confirmation from Apple that a purchase is active so it can remove ads.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices

  • Turn off ad tracking in iOS Settings, or reset your advertising identifier.
  • Remove banner ads and ads between levels with a Premium subscription.
  • Delete the App to remove all game data stored on your device.

Data retention and security

Because game data stays on your device, we do not retain it. Information collected by Google for advertising is handled under Google's privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
